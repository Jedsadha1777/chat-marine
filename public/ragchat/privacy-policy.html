RAGChat Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-01 · Jedsadha Rojanaphan · ipanda1777@gmail.com App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6806576444

RAGChat runs on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of ours.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no networking code, no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking library, and it asks for no permissions. Your questions, chats and documents are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Two things can still reach us, and neither contains your chats or documents: an email you send us, which we use only to reply to you, and what Apple shows every developer — crash reports if you turned on Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers, and the text of any App Store review you write.

What stays on your device

Your chats, your imported documents, and the assistant's persona and mood settings are stored in the app's own storage on your device.

Imported documents are copied into the app's KnowledgeBase folder, which you can browse in the Files app — anyone who can unlock your device can read them there.

Because this is ordinary app data, it is included in your iCloud or computer backups according to your own settings.

How to delete it

Deleting a document in the app removes the file and everything built from it. Deleting a chat removes that chat and its messages. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

On-device processing

Answers are produced by Apple's on-device model. Your question, the passages found in your own documents, your persona settings and the earlier turns of the same chat are put into the prompt for that model. Apple documents this model as running on the device.

Replies, and questions written in a language the model does not support, may be translated automatically using Apple's Translation framework.

The app makes no network connections of its own, but iOS fetches Apple's own assets on its behalf: the language model, translation language packs, and text-embedding assets the first time the app launches. None of those carry your content.

Third-party code

The app includes GRDB and ZIPFoundation (MIT licence) and one Material Symbols icon (Apache 2.0). They run entirely on your device and collect nothing. Their licence texts are shown in the app under Settings → About.

Children

RAGChat is not directed at children, and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

This page is the current version. There is no account and no mailing list, so any change takes effect when it is published here.
