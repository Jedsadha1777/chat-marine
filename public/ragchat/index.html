What RAGChat needs to work

An iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on, running iOS 26 or later.

Turn it on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri, then let the model finish downloading — keep Wi-Fi and power connected. Until that finishes, RAGChat can show your documents but cannot answer.

Common questions

The app says the AI isn't available. Apple Intelligence is off, still downloading, or this iPhone doesn't support it. Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri will tell you which.

It answers in the wrong language, or says it doesn't understand. The model has its own language setting, separate from your iPhone's: Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri → Language. If your language isn't on Apple's list, ask in English — RAGChat translates replies into the language you choose in its own Settings.

It says the answer isn't in my documents, but I know it is. Two things to check. First, each chat searches only the documents ticked under "•••" → Knowledge. Second, try asking with the words the document actually uses — asking for "the travel budget" when the document says "per diem" can miss.

How do I add documents? Knowledge → "+" → Paste Text, or Import File (.txt, .csv, .xlsx, .docx, .pdf). The paperclip next to the message box does both at once: it adds the file and points your next question at it.

Where are my files, and how do I remove them? In the Files app under RAGChat. Deleting a document inside the app removes it and everything built from it; deleting the app removes everything.

Things to know

Answers come from an AI model running on your iPhone. They can be wrong, incomplete or out of date — even when they point at your own documents. Check anything that matters against the original.
